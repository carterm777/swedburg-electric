# Design direction — Swedburg Electric

Assigned so that no two of the 25 sites in this campaign converge on the same
look. These are **starting positions with authority**, not idle suggestions: build
from them. You may deviate from any one of them, but the deviation costs you one
line of written justification in `SELECTION-LOG.md` explaining what the assigned
option lost against and why — the same discipline `section-style-repo.md` asks
for everywhere else.

**Primary style family:** editorial
**Page ambition level:** restrained-and-quiet — the most luxurious page in the batch

## The specific risk on this brief

Serif display over a serif body, deep teal-black with brass. This is the closest thing here to a heritage-brand or hospitality site, and it should feel like one. Very generous margins, a narrow measure, brass used once or twice. No cards with drop shadows anywhere.

## Signature element — the photo-diagnosis widget

Card-Based Widget Container, editorial — brass hairlines, letterpress-quiet, no visible drop zone dashes

This is the most designed thing on the page. If a stranger screenshotted one
element from this site, it should be this one.

## Carry these through the page

- **Image technique to feature:** Grid-Breaking Oversized Image — from `references/image-and-visual-richness.md`. Use it at least twice, deliberately.
- **Motion technique to feature:** Clip Reveal on section titles, Depth Settle on photography — from `references/animation-and-motion-richness.md`.

Both are on top of, not instead of, the per-section choices above. Read the real
entry in the reference file before implementing either — the Avoid-when field
applies to you.
